What Your Donation Does

We make sure every donation reaches those who need it. Here's what different giving levels achieve:

🍱
$25 / ₦20,000 Feeds a family of 5 for one full week through our food relief program.
📚
$50 / ₦40,000 Provides a full set of school textbooks and stationery for one student.
🏥
$100 / ₦80,000 Sponsors a free medical consultation and medications for 10 patients.
🎓
$250 / ₦200,000 Pays a full term's school fees for one scholarship beneficiary.
🏠
$500 / ₦400,000 Helps repair the roof of a widow's home or supports emergency shelter for a family.
🌍
$1,000+ / ₦800,000+ Funds an entire community outreach event — medical, food, and education combined.
