Who We Are

A Foundation Built on Compassion and Purpose

Care & More Charity Foundation was established to be the bridge between generosity and genuine need. We believe every person deserves dignity, opportunity, and a chance at a better life.

From education support to emergency relief, our programs are designed to address root causes, not just symptoms, creating pathways out of vulnerability for individuals and entire communities.

What We Do

Programs That Change Lives

Our work spans six core areas, each carefully designed to meet the real, pressing needs of the communities we serve.

🎓

Education & Scholarships

We fund school fees, supply learning materials, and provide mentorship to students who would otherwise be left behind.

🏥

Healthcare Outreach

Free medical camps, health screenings, and maternal care programs bring medical attention to those who cannot access it.

🍲

Food Relief Programs

Weekly food distributions and nutrition programs ensure families in crisis never go to bed hungry.

🏠

Shelter & Housing

Emergency housing support and home rebuilding projects restore safety and stability to displaced families.

👩

Women Empowerment

Skills acquisition, microfinance access, and mentorship programs help women build economic independence.

🌱

Youth Development

Vocational training, sports programs, and leadership camps invest in the next generation of community leaders.
